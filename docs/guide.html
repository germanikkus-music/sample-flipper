SAMPLEFLIPPER Germanikkus · Quick guide

Load a sample, chop it, flip the chops, build a new beat, and take it to your MPC Live III. The colors match the areas marked on pages 2 and 3.

The workflow

1Load

Drop a WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A or MP4 on the page, or press Load sample.

Top right

2Tempo

BPM comes from the file name (Splice: OS_ES_90_…) or is detected. Fix it with ½, ×2 or Tap.

Sequencer bar

3Chop

Too long? Select a range, then Keep or Cut. Auto-chop on hits, equal slices, or tap along. Drag markers to fine-tune.

Waveform

4Play pads

Every chop lands on a pad, A01 bottom-left like the MPC. Click, tap or use the keyboard.

Pads

5Flip

Per chop: reverse, pitch, gain, fade in/out, choke. Changes are heard right away.

Flip panel

6Sequence

Click steps, or let Flip it rearrange the chops in one of 7 styles. 1 to 4 bars. Space plays.

Step grid

7Export

Chops as WAVs + MPC drum program + MPC pattern, or the beat as one WAV.

Export panel

8MPC

Copy the folder to the MPC, load the .xpm, import the .mpcpattern.

MPC Live III

From the app to the MPC

Chops .zip contains one folder

MySample/MySample_01.wav … _16.wav
MySample/MySample.xpm drum program
MySample/MySample 90bpm.mpcpattern

Flips are baked into the WAVs. 16- or 24-bit, same sample rate as your file.

➜

Copy to the MPC

  1. Unzip on your Mac.
  2. Copy the whole folder to an SD card, USB drive or the MPC's drive.
  3. Keep the WAVs next to the .xpm: it finds them by name.
➜

On the Live III

  1. Browser → load MySample.xpm. Chop 1 = pad A01.
  2. Import the .mpcpattern into the track using that program.
  3. Or just drop Pattern .wav on a track as an audio loop.
