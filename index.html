Export

Chops export with their flips baked in. Unzip, copy the folder to your MPC, and load the .xpm: chop 1 is pad A01. The .mpcpattern (length set by Bars) can be imported into a track that uses that program. The pattern .wav renders the sequence at the current BPM for the chosen number of bars (it loops if that's longer than the pattern), ready to loop.
